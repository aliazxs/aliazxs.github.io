Privacy policy

Wuddi is a family requests app by Ali Almohsin in Al-Ahsa. We collect name, username, email, hashed password, optional phone, profile and item photos, circle membership, requests, and routines. Biometrics stay on your device. We do not sell your data. Circle members see your name, photo, and that circle’s requests — not your phone number. Delete your account from Profile. Contact: aliazxs@hotmail.com

Support

Email aliazxs@hotmail.com from the address on your account. To delete your account, use Delete account in Wuddi, or write to us if you cannot open the app.
